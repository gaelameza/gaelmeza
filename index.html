Case studies

By the numbers.

01 / TonyMakesVlogs

6.9M views for the cost of one pair of boots.

Goal
Grow reach for the national account with no budget.
What I did
Spotted a creator mid-way through his "Becoming A Pro Soccer Player" series, playing in Copa Mundial boots with holes in them. Pitched the collab myself, coordinated the product, and ran it as a collab post so the reach credited Soccer Post.
Result
6.9M views, 579K likes, 53.4K shares, 19.6K saves: about 240x the account's ~29K followers, and the most-viewed post in company history. It also proved a format. A simple boot unboxing on a trending audio is now one of our content pillars (see the unboxings in Work).
What I'd do next
Funnel the new audience straight to the website: a CTA in the creator's caption, and a pinned post that tells new viewers who we are.

02 / YouTube revival

Dormant for a decade. 130K+ views in a month.

Before~10 yearsof near-flat views and only occasional uploads
After130K+ viewsand ~300 new subscribers in 30 days
Goal
Bring back a channel that had been effectively dormant for a decade.
What I did
Took full ownership as YouTube account manager: rebranded the channel, launched our YouTube Community, and published its first post, which beat our engagement expectations.
Result
130K+ views and ~300 new subscribers in the 30 days to Oct 14, 2026. That is nearly 9% of the channel's 1.5M lifetime views, in a single month. September 2026 (~80K views) was its best month in about 15 years.
What I'd do next
Post quality long-form horizontal video on a steady schedule, built to be cut down and reused across our other platforms.

03 / World Cup content program

~800K views in six weeks.

Goal
Own the World Cup moment across the national and Austin accounts.
What I did
Concepted, researched, fact-checked, and produced the program: SP Stats carousels (Mexico and USMNT, Round of 16), a quarterfinals bracket, matchup graphics, and boot and kit content, all on a verify-before-publish rule.
Result
June 9 to July 21, 2026, national and Austin combined: ~800K views, ~266K accounts reached, ~40K interactions, ~994 net new followers.
What I'd do next
Have a breakout plan ready. When one reel outperforms, ride it the same week: a fast follow-up in the same format, a pinned post, and a short series built on what worked.
