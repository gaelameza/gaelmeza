01 / TonyMakesVlogs

6.9M views. Cost: one pair of boots.

Goal
Grow reach for the national account with no budget.
What I did
Spotted a creator mid-way through his "Becoming A Pro Soccer Player" series, playing in Copa Mundial boots with holes in them. Pitched the collab myself, coordinated the product, and ran it as a collab post so the reach credited Soccer Post.
Result
6.9M views, 579K likes, 53.4K shares, 19.6K saves. About 240x the account's ~29K followers at the time. Most-viewed content in company history. It was my first paid creator collab, and it showed me that a simple boot unboxing with a trending audio works, so I made that a content pillar.
What I'd do next
Funnel the new audience straight to the website: a CTA in the creator's caption, and a pinned post that tells new viewers who we are.

02 / YouTube revival

130K+ views in 30 days.

Goal
Revive a channel that had barely posted in over a decade. Others had tried before me.
What I did
Took over as YouTube account manager and ran all of it: a new channel banner, our first YouTube Community, and our first Community post, which drew surprisingly strong engagement.
Result
Over 130,000 views and almost 300 new subscribers in the 30 days to Oct 14, 2026. September 2026, at about 80,000 views, was the channel's best month in roughly 15 years.
What I'd do next
Post quality long-form horizontal video on a steady schedule, built to be cut down and reused across our other platforms.

04 / Studio shoot

Nike Tiempo x Jordan SE. One boot, a full studio set.

Goal
Launch content for every Soccer Post market account, led by Austin.
What I did
Lit, shot, and edited the whole set myself. Stills on a clean red backdrop with a single gridded key light: hero shots, macro details of the leather, soleplate, and heel, and styled flat lays, edited in Lightroom. The vertical video uses one key light on a dark background to match the edge of the boot, shot by me and edited in DaVinci Resolve.
Result
Other market accounts chose my assets, so the set ran across the network, and the video went out on the national account. The Austin stores saw a big jump in in-store traffic.
